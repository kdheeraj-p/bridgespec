# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous project. Be welcoming,
assume good faith, criticize ideas and measurements rather than people, and do
not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about
another contributor.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, off-topic, or privacy-
violating content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile or security-advisory channel.
